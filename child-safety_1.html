Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Duelify has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and for child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page sets out the standards we apply, how we enforce them, and how to report a concern.

1. Our Prohibition

The following are strictly prohibited on Duelify and are grounds for immediate and permanent account termination:

  • Any content that sexualises, exploits, or endangers a minor;
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form;
  • Grooming, solicitation, or any attempt to establish inappropriate contact with a minor;
  • Sexualised commentary about minors, including on otherwise ordinary images;
  • Sharing, requesting, or linking to CSAE material anywhere on or through the service.

These prohibitions are also stated in our Terms of Service.

2. Age Requirements

Users must be at least 16 years old to create a Duelify account. Accounts we identify as belonging to users under this age are removed. If you believe an underage user has created an account, please contact us at the address below.

3. In-App Reporting

Every post, comment, and user profile on Duelify can be reported from within the app in one tap, with child safety available as a reporting reason. Users can also block any other user immediately.

Reports are recorded and reviewed. Content that receives reports from multiple distinct users is automatically hidden from all other users pending review, so harmful material stops circulating before a human has reviewed it.

4. Our Response

When we identify or receive a report of CSAE content, we:

  • Remove the content;
  • Permanently terminate the account or accounts responsible;
  • Preserve relevant records as required for investigation;
  • Report to the relevant regional or national authorities, including the Australian Centre to Counter Child Exploitation (ACCCE) and, where applicable, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

We comply with all applicable child safety laws in the jurisdictions where Duelify is available.

5. Reporting to Authorities Directly

If you believe a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

  • Australia: Australian Centre to Counter Child Exploitation — accce.gov.au/report
  • United States: NCMEC CyberTipline — report.cybertip.org
  • United Kingdom: Internet Watch Foundation — iwf.org.uk
