# Privacy-Policy

Duelify — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25/07/2026

What we collect. When you use Duelify we collect your email address, username, display name, optional profile photo, and the content you create — posts, votes, comments, messages, and stories — plus your in-app settings and social connections (follows, blocks, reports).

What we don't do. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, we don't use tracking or advertising SDKs, and we don't collect your precise location or contacts.

How it's used. Solely to operate the app: showing your content to others according to your privacy settings, delivering messages and notifications, enforcing our community rules, and responding to support requests.

Where it lives. Data is stored securely on Supabase, our cloud backend provider, protected by access rules that enforce your privacy settings. Direct messages are visible only to conversation participants. Stories expire after 24 hours.

Your control. You can edit your details anytime, delete anything you've posted, deactivate your account (reversible), or permanently delete your account — which erases all your data, including uploaded images, immediately and irreversibly.

Age. Duelify is for users 13 and over.

Questions? Contact us at duelify@duelifyapp.com. Full policy: duelifyapp.com/privacy.html
