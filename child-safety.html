Privacy Policy Effective date: 6 November 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how Duelify ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and related services. By using Duelify, you agree to the practices described in this policy. 1. Information We Collect When you create an account and use Duelify, we collect: Account information: your email address, username, display name, and password (stored in encrypted form by our authentication provider — we never see your plain-text password). Profile information: your profile photo (avatar) and bio, if you choose to add them. Content you create: posts (including images and captions), votes, comments, replies, likes, direct messages, and stories. Social activity: who you follow, who follows you, users you block, and content you save or report. Settings and preferences: your privacy settings (such as who can comment or vote on your posts), muted words, and app preferences like theme choice. We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. 2. How We Use Your Information We use your information to: Provide and operate the App — showing your posts, delivering messages, counting votes, and displaying notifications. Enforce your privacy settings (for example, restricting who can vote or comment on your posts). Keep the community safe — reviewing reported content and enforcing our Terms of Service. Respond to your support requests. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for advertising. 3. Where Your Data Is Stored Duelify is built on Supabase, a third-party backend platform that hosts our database, authentication, and file storage. Your data is stored on Supabase's secure infrastructure and protected by row-level security rules that restrict access according to your privacy settings. You can read Supabase's own privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy. 4. Who Can See Your Content Posts, comments, votes, and stories are visible to other users of the App according to your account and per-post privacy settings. Direct messages are visible only to the participants of the conversation. Stories automatically expire and stop being visible 24 hours after posting. If you set your vote history to private, other users cannot see the posts you have voted on. 5. Your Rights and Controls You are in control of your data. Within the App you can: Edit your profile, username, display name, email, and password at any time in Settings. Delete individual posts, comments, messages, and stories you have created. Deactivate your account (Settings → Deactivate Account). While deactivated, your profile and content are hidden from other users. Logging back in reactivates your account. Permanently delete your account (Settings → Delete Account Permanently). This immediately and irreversibly erases your account, profile, posts, votes, comments, messages, stories, uploaded images, and all associated data from our systems. Block other users and report content that violates our guidelines. If you need help exercising any of these rights, contact us at duelify@duelifyapp.com. 6. Data Retention We keep your data only for as long as your account exists. When you permanently delete your account, all of your data — including uploaded images in storage — is erased. Deactivating your account hides your data but retains it so you can return. Expired stories are no longer displayed after 24 hours. 7. Security We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted password storage, and database-level access rules that prevent users from accessing data they are not permitted to see. No system is perfectly secure, so we encourage you to use a strong, unique password. 8. Children Duelify is not directed at children under 16, and you must be at least 16 years old to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, contact us at duelify@duelifyapp.com and we will delete it. 9. International Users Duelify is operated from Australia. By using the App, you understand that your information may be stored and processed on servers located outside your country of residence. We handle personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), and we aim to honour data rights available to users under other applicable laws (such as the GDPR for users in the EU/UK), including the rights of access, correction, and deletion described above. 10. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and may notify you in the App. Continued use of Duelify after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy. 11. Contact Us For any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at duelify@duelifyapp.com.
